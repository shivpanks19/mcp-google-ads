- **Hero:** “India’s First Agentic Interactive Flat Panel (AIFP™)” and “Powering leading institutions across **Maharashtra**.”

### Content structure (funnel)

1. **Hero + value prop** → demo / brochure.
2. **Product tiles:** A10 Pro (“True AI Digital Board”), A20 EDLA (“Google Certified AI Digital Board”).
3. **Problem → solution:** Teachers juggling tools → unified system for Maharashtra.
4. **Feature grid:** 3D + AI copilot, Lab in Board, mind mapping, slides generator, fiducial polling, built-in curriculum (CBSE/ICSE/state), offline AI, device management / broadcast, admin reporting.
5. **Social proof:** “Success Stories from **Maharashtra’s** Top Schools” with three named quotes (DY Patil Vidyapeeth, Vidya Pratishthan, Indus International).
6. **Closing CTA:** “Join Schools in **Maharashtra** Already Using Cybernetyx AIFP™.”
7. **Lead capture:** Multi-step demo form (category, budget bands in ₹), urgency (“only 5 Demo Slots left this week”), duplicate brochure blocks (name / phone / email / category / budget).
8. **Footer stats:** 80+ countries, 15mn+ users, 10K+ organisations.

### Messaging notes

- **Brand mix:** Page title/URL say **EyeRIS**; body heavily uses **Cybernetyx AIFP™** and **BrightClass** — clear for insiders, but worth checking whether prospects confuse OEM vs. product brand.
- **Numbers:** Maharashtra uses “1,500+ schools”; the national [products root](https://products.eyerisinteractive.com/) uses “**15,000+** Institutions” — different metrics; ensure consistency or add footnotes to avoid skepticism.

## Critical content bug (Bangalore / template)

On the **Bangalore** page, several blocks still reference **Maharashtra** despite Karnataka-localized headings:

- Success stories intro: “what educators across **Maharashtra** have to say.”
- Closing section: “Join Schools in **Maharashtra** Already Using Cybernetyx AIFP™.”

Testimonials cite **DY Patil Vidyapeeth**, **Vidya Pratishthan**, **Indus International** — institutions associated with **Maharashtra**, not Karnataka. For Bangalore SEO and trust, this **hurts relevance** and can read as copy-paste errors.

**Recommendation:** Parameterize region name, CTA headings, and testimonials per region (or use national quotes on geo pages until local proof exists).

## SEO and UX observations

- **Strengths:** Clear H1/H2 hierarchy, repeated CTAs, ₹ budget segmentation, syllabus alignment, offline AI (important for India), concrete school names on Maharashtra page.
- **Risks:** Duplicate “Get the brochure” sections; repeated “Processing your submission…” may look broken if visible before submit; **geo pages** need unique body copy and local proof to avoid **duplicate content** vs. root and vs. each other.
- **Trust:** Align institution counts; clarify EyeRIS vs. Cybernetyx vs. BrightClass in one line early on.
